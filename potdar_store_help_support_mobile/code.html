Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my order? expand_more
You can track your order status in the 'Orders' section of your account. Once your order is dispatched, you will receive a tracking link via SMS and email.
How do I return an item? expand_more
To return an item, go to your order history, select the order containing the item, and tap 'Return Item'. Follow the instructions to schedule a pickup. Perishable items cannot be returned.
What payment methods do you accept? expand_more
We accept all major credit and debit cards, UPI, popular digital wallets, and Cash on Delivery for eligible areas.
How do I change my delivery address? expand_more
You can manage your delivery addresses in the 'Account' section. If you need to change the address for an active order, please contact support immediately.
